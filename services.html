Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost?

Cost depends on the customer journey, content, integrations, languages, and ongoing responsibilities. We confirm a written scope and quote after understanding what the website must achieve.

Can you help NGOs and local businesses?

Absolutely. We have experience working with NGOs, SMEs, and startups, providing affordable and effective web solutions.

What is Core Web Vitals and why does it matter?

Core Web Vitals measure loading speed, responsiveness, and visual stability. Improving them supports a better customer experience and removes technical friction, but no single metric guarantees search rankings.

Do you provide SEO and ongoing support?

Yes, we offer SEO setup, audits, and ongoing support to help your website grow and succeed beyond launch. Support subscriptions also include a private dashboard where you can check your subscription details and current service status whenever you want.
